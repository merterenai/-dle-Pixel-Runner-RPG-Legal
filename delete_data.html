Delete Your Data

Idle Pixel Runner RPG · Bagatone Interactive

Option 1 - In the game (fastest)

Open the Game, go to Settings, and tap Delete My Data. This permanently erases your local save, your Google Play Games cloud save and, from version 1.0.0.6, your game account and all related records on our servers, including leaderboard and battle records. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 - By email (if you can no longer open the Game)

You can ask us to delete the player ID and all server-side records linked to you. Email us from the address below and include your display name (or, if you registered with email, the email address of your account) and roughly when you last played, so we can find your account. We may ask for extra details to confirm the request is yours. We aim to respond within 30 days.

bagatoneinteractive@gmail.com

What gets deleted, and what may remain

  • Deleted: your game account (including your email address and password hash, if you registered with email), progress and gameplay records, leaderboard and battle records, and analytics events linked to you on our servers.
  • Encrypted backups may keep deleted data for up to 35 days before being permanently overwritten.
  • Data held by Google (Play Games, Firebase, AdMob, Google Play purchase records) is governed by Google. Manage it at myaccount.google.com.
  • We may keep the minimum information required by law or to prevent abuse (for example that an account was banned for cheating).

See our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details on what data we collect and why.
