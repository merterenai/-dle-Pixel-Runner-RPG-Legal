DELETE YOUR DATA

How to delete everything Idle Pixel Runner RPG keeps about you.

01 IN THE GAME (FASTEST)

Open the Game, go to Settings, and tap Delete My Data. This permanently erases:

  • your save on the device;
  • from version 1.0.6, your game account and every record linked to it on our servers: friends and blocks, friendly and ranked battle records, ranked points and history, rewards, and redeemed codes;
  • the analytics events sent from that installation.

This cannot be undone. If our servers cannot be reached at that moment, the Game retries the server deletion the next time it can.

02 BY EMAIL (IF YOU CAN NO LONGER OPEN THE GAME)

Email us at the address below with your in-game name or friend code (shown in the Social screen) and roughly when you last played, so we can find your account. We may ask for details to confirm the account is yours. We answer within 30 days.

03 WHAT MAY REMAIN

  • Encrypted backups may keep deleted data for up to 35 days before it is overwritten.
  • If an account was banned for cheating, a keyed one-way hash of its identifier is kept to prevent the same abuse from returning. Nothing else is kept.
  • Data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play purchase records, and a cloud save made by an earlier version of the Game with Google Play Games Saved Games, which the Game no longer uses) is managed by Google at https://myaccount.google.com.
  • Accounts with no activity for 24 months are deleted automatically in the same way.
