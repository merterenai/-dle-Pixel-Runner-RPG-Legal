PRIVACY POLICY

Bagatone Interactive ("we," "us," "our") publishes the mobile game Idle Pixel Runner RPG (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. The online features described here (game accounts, friends, clans, friendly battles, ranked battles, leaderboards, redeem codes and our own analytics) apply from version 1.0.6 of the Game; earlier versions do not use our servers.

01 SUMMARY

  • The core game works offline and your progress is saved on your device.
  • We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or a password.
  • To run the online features, our own servers keep a game account for you. It is created automatically with a random identifier; if you sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games identity is linked to it.
  • Other players can see your in-game name, friend code (if you share it), and battle and ranking information. The Game has no chat or messaging.
  • Analytics and crash reports are sent only with your consent where the law requires it, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • You can delete everything from Settings > Delete My Data.
  • We do not sell your data.

02 INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Save data on your device

Your progress (stage, level, currencies, items, upgrades, allies, skills, goals) and your settings (sound, notifications, language, analytics choice) are stored on your device.

b) Google Play Games cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, a backup copy of your save is stored on Google's servers using the Play Games Saved Games service, so you can continue on another device. Google holds this backup under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

c) Your game account on our servers

  • Account identifier: a random identifier created by our server. Your device keeps a random secret key for it; our server stores only a one-way hash of that key.
  • Play Games link: if you sign in with Google Play Games, we store a keyed one-way hash of your Play Games player ID (not the ID itself) so you can recover your account on a new device.
  • In-game name and friend code: your name is generated automatically (for example "Guest-A1B2") and your friend code is random. You can change your name once in Settings; names must follow our rules and be unique. We keep your earlier names (up to 10) with your account, so that no one else can take them and so that we can handle reports; they are deleted together with your account.
  • Game profile and progress: level, highest stage, and the hero statistics used in battles (for example damage, health, allies and skills), plus the progress reports the Game sends so our server can check that progress is genuine.
  • Friends: your friends list, friend requests (sent and received), and players you have blocked.
  • Clans: the clan you belong to (its name, code and level), your role in it and when you joined. A clan is a group of up to 30 players; it has no chat. Its level is worked out on our server from the highest stages its members have reached. When you leave, are removed from a clan or delete your data you leave the clan, and a clan without members is deleted. Clan names follow the same rules as player names.
  • Save backup: the Game sends a copy of your whole save (the same data that is stored on your device, without device-only items such as the analytics queue) to our server when you open it and when it goes to the background. We keep the latest 10 copies and one per day for the last 7 days. It lets us restore your progress after a loss or a technical fault, and, if you ask us for help, put an earlier copy back on your device. Only the game operator can read or restore it, and it is deleted with your account.
  • Battles: results of friendly battles between friends, and for ranked battles your points, rank, wins, losses and draws, your match history (the last 50 matches, including opponents' in-game names), a hidden skill rating used for matchmaking, and the ranked rewards you have received. While a battle runs, our server also receives the moments you use your skills; these are only kept until the battle ends.
  • Redeem codes: which codes your account has used.
  • Reports: if you report another player, the reason you picked; if someone reports you, their report. Reports are reviewed by us.
  • Fair-play records: if our checks flag your account as suspicious, or if an account is banned for cheating, we keep a record of that decision.

d) Analytics (with your consent where required)

The Game sends gameplay events (for example stage cleared, upgrade bought, session length, battle played) to our own servers under a random installation identifier that is not linked to your game account, together with the app version, device model and Android version. We use these events only to improve balance and features.

e) Crash and error reports (with your consent where required)

If the Game crashes or hits an error, a technical report (error details, the last in-game steps before the error, app version, device model, Android version) is sent to our servers. Reports are filtered so that they do not contain your name, friend code or save data.

f) Server logs

Our servers record technical information about requests: the time, the request type, the result, the app version, a shortened form of your IP address (the last part is removed), and a pseudonymous form of your account identifier. We use these logs for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Your full IP address is used only in memory, to limit how many requests one connection can make.

g) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads; you choose when to watch one, and there are no forced ads. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your ad choices first.

h) In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of a completed purchase so we can grant it.

03 WHAT OTHER PLAYERS CAN SEE

  • Friends see your in-game name, level, highest stage, battle profile and your battle record against them, and you appear on their friends leaderboard.
  • In ranked battles your opponent sees your in-game name and rank. The ranked ladder shows the in-game names, ranks and points of the top players (and your own place) to all players.
  • Members of your clan see your in-game name, level, highest stage and role in the clan, and can compare their profile with yours, report you, or invite you to a friendly battle. Anyone who knows a clan's code can join it while it has room.
  • Anyone you give your friend code to can send you a friend request. You can decline requests, remove friends, and block players.

Your email address, phone number and location are never shown. The Game has no chat or messaging.

04 HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To provide the Game and its online features: accounts, friends, battles, matchmaking, rankings, rewards and redeem codes.
  • To keep play fair and safe: checking progress and battle results, detecting cheating and abuse, handling reports, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To keep the service running: security, troubleshooting, and fixing crashes and errors.
  • To improve the Game: balance and feature decisions based on analytics.

We do not profile you beyond matching you with opponents of similar skill and our fair-play checks. Automatic checks can flag an account as suspicious, which pauses its ranked rewards and ladder place until we review it; bans are decided by a person.

05 SHARING AND WHO PROCESSES YOUR DATA

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with:

  • our hosting provider, Keyubu, which runs our servers on our behalf;
  • Google, for Play Games, Play Billing and AdMob, each under Google's own terms;
  • authorities, when the law requires it.

06 WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

Our servers and their backups are located in Turkiye. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or elsewhere, using the online features means your data is transferred to and processed in Turkiye, which is necessary to provide the features you choose to use. Data handled by Google may be processed in other countries under Google's terms.

07 LEGAL BASES

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK (Turkiye's Law No. 6698) or similar laws apply, we rely on:

  • performance of our service to you: your account, friends, battles, rankings, rewards and redeem codes;
  • our legitimate interests: security, fair play and anti-cheat, handling reports, server logs, and keeping a record of bans;
  • your consent: analytics and crash reports where consent is required, and ad personalization; you can withdraw it at any time;
  • legal obligations, where they apply.

08 HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA

  • Save data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud save (Google): until you use Delete My Data or delete it in your Google account.
  • Save backup copies on our servers: the latest 10 and one per day for 7 days, and all of them are deleted with your account.
  • Game account and everything linked to it (friends, clan membership, battles, rankings, rewards, redeemed codes): while you use it; deleted automatically after 24 months without any activity, or immediately when you use Delete My Data.
  • Friend requests: 30 days if not answered.
  • Analytics events on our servers: up to 14 months.
  • Crash and error reports on our servers: up to 180 days.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups: up to 35 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too.
  • Ban records: a keyed one-way hash of a banned account's identifier is kept to prevent the same abuse from returning, even after the account is deleted.
  • Deletion markers: when we delete an account (for example at your request by email), we keep only a keyed one-way hash of its identifier for 24 months, so that the Game on your phone can recognise the deletion and delete its save too.
  • Google services keep data under Google's own retention rules.

09 YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

  • Delete My Data (Settings): permanently erases your save on this device, your Play Games cloud save, your game account and every record linked to it on our servers, and the analytics events sent under this installation's identifier. It cannot be undone. If our servers cannot be reached at that moment, the Game retries the server deletion the next time it can.
  • Delete without the Game: email us at the address in Section 13 with your in-game name or friend code; we may ask for details to confirm the account is yours, and we answer within 30 days. When we delete your account this way, the Game on your phone also deletes its save (and your Play Games cloud save) the next time it connects to our servers.
  • Analytics and crash reports: switch them off at any time in Settings > Analytics.
  • Ads: manage ad personalization in your Android settings (Google > Ads) and, where shown, in Google's consent form.
  • Google data: manage what Google holds at https://myaccount.google.com.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or Turkiye), you also have the right to access your data, correct it, receive a copy, restrict or object to processing, and complain to your data protection authority (in Turkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). California residents have similar rights to know, delete and correct; we do not sell personal information, and ads served through AdMob may count as "sharing" for advertising under California law, which you can limit through your ad personalization settings. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 13.

10 CHILDREN

The Game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The online features are intended for players aged 16 or older, or the minimum age required by law in your country if higher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11 SECURITY

Traffic between the Game and our servers is encrypted (TLS) and the Game checks our server's certificate. Access to our servers is restricted, the management tools are not reachable from the internet, and backups are encrypted. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

12 CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

When we change what the Game collects or how it is used, we update this policy, change the "Last updated" date, and ask you to accept the new version when you next open the Game.
